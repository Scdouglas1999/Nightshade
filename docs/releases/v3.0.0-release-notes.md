# Nightshade 3.0.0 — What's New

**Channel:** `beta` · **Version:** `3.0.0` (build `7`)

Nightshade 3.0.0 is the biggest release yet. The centerpiece is a brand-new **live imaging cockpit** that turns the Dashboard into the one screen you sit on all night — but it's joined by one-click night planning, a fully-rebuilt plate solver, a reorganized and searchable Settings, a denser and more capable imaging view, planetarium upgrades, and a long tail of fixes and polish. This is a detailed tour of everything new.

---

## ✨ The Dashboard is now a live imaging cockpit

The Dashboard used to be a glance. Now it's mission control — a NINA-style cockpit you can watch your entire session unfold on, without bouncing between screens.

**It wakes up when you do.** The moment you connect gear or load a sequence, the cockpit comes alive and fills in with live data — the current target, your equipment's state, the running exposure. When nothing is set up yet, it shows a calm standby card instead of a wall of empty panels: a one-tap **Plan Tonight** button, a summary of your last run, and a readiness glance showing which devices are connected and your cooler temperature.

**Everything you need, at a glance, in one place:**

- **Now Imaging** — a compact status strip across the top: the active target with its coordinates, current **altitude** (color-coded as it climbs or sinks), **time to meridian**, the running exposure's **countdown**, and overall **sequence progress** with a live ETA.
- **Frames** — the most recent sub displayed up top, with a horizontal strip of your latest captures docked beneath it (each thumbnail labeled with its filter, exposure, and time). Before the first frame lands it collapses to a slim "waiting for first frame" line so it never wastes space.
- **Equipment telemetry** — camera cooling and power, mount state, focuser position, filter wheel, rotator, and guider, all live.
- **Guiding** — the RA/Dec guiding trace with live RMS.
- **Weather & safety** — current conditions and any active safety alerts.
- **Trigger feed** — your sequence's watchdogs (HFR, guiding loss, time windows, drift) reporting as they fire, with timestamps.
- **Per-filter integration** — how much time you've banked on each filter for the running target.

**Run your night without leaving the screen.** Inline controls let you **Start** a loaded sequence (with the usual pre-flight checks), **Pause/Resume**, **Skip** the current step, and **Stop**. Stop is deliberately *hold-to-confirm* so a stray click can't abort a six-hour run at 3 a.m.

**Nothing important scrolls away.** A critical-event banner and a recovery banner pin to the very top, so a weather-triggered park or an in-progress recovery loop is always visible — and those same critical events now also reach your notifications.

**It's compact on purpose.** Related panels were merged so both columns fit without endless scrolling: the live frame and the recent-frames strip are one tile, and the target header and exposure progress are one tile.

**Make it yours.** Drag tiles to reorder them, move them between zones, resize them, and show or hide anything from the customize menu. Power users can switch on optional panels — the target **scheduler**, a live **cloud-motion** readout, an **adaptive-conditions** score, and a live **light-curve** plot for photometry runs. When you upgrade, your existing Dashboard layout is migrated to the new cockpit automatically; if you'd rather start fresh, reset it from the customize menu.

> The Sequencer's old "Run" tab is gone — everything it did now lives on the Dashboard. The Sequencer is for *building* sequences; the Dashboard is for *running and watching* them.

## 🌙 Plan Tonight — one-click night planning

The **Smart Night** planner builds a complete imaging plan for you from your gear and the sky tonight, in one short wizard:

1. **Window** — confirms tonight's dark window from your location's astronomical twilight (narrow it if you don't want the whole night).
2. **Equipment** — uses your active profile (camera, OTA, mount, filters), with options like cool-to temperature and end-of-night flats.
3. **Targets** — auto-picks the top targets by score for tonight's conditions, or lets you hand-pick from the ranked list.
4. **Strategy** — auto LRGB, mono LRGB, narrowband HOO/SHO, or one-shot-color, matched to the filters you actually have.
5. **Preview** — a full timeline and per-target / per-filter breakdown you can tweak (frame counts, sub length, SNR target, autofocus cadence, integration budget) before you accept.
6. **Go** — the generated sequence loads into the editor (and can start immediately and drop you on the Dashboard to watch).

It accounts for real-world context — light pollution, cloud probability, polar-alignment freshness, and gaps in your dark library — and it can auto-schedule missing calibration. There's also a **dashboard prompt** that offers to build tonight's plan as soon as your hardware is ready.

**Plan a campaign, not just a night.** New **multi-night projects** pair with an Open-Meteo **"best nights this week"** forecast, so you can aim a target at the actual upcoming weather window instead of guessing.

## 🎯 The Planner is your target HQ

- The **Planner is now the single home** for tonight's recommendations — a scored, filterable list of what's up and worth shooting, each with an altitude curve, integration estimate, and the reasoning behind its score.
- **Every candidate can go straight to the Sequencer.** Previously only the top-ranked target had a "Review in Sequencer" action; now *every* recommendation does, so you can take any target into the editor in one click.
- You can also send any target to **Framing** or add it to an **observing list**, and search beyond your local catalog (installed catalogs + SIMBAD name resolution) right from the list.
- The redundant **Framing "Suggestions" tab was removed** — there's now one clear place to answer "what should I shoot tonight," not two.

## 🔭 Plate solving — fixed, end to end

Plate solving with ASTAP has been rebuilt and is now dependable:

- It **honors the ASTAP binary and catalog folder you configured** instead of silently auto-detecting something else. (If you set a custom path before, re-confirm it in **Settings → Plate Solving**.)
- It **no longer modifies your light frames.** Solving now writes a separate sidecar file; the previous behavior could overwrite your own capture data.
- It **passes ASTAP its star-catalog directory**, so solves don't fail for a missing database.
- It **reads ASTAP's actual success/failure result** instead of assuming success, and surfaces the real reason when a solve fails.
- It **enforces the solve timeout**, so a hung solve can't stall your sequence indefinitely.

If centering, sync, or annotation-by-solve had been failing for you, this release should clear it up — and sequences that depend on a solve to start will start.

## 📷 Imaging & capture

- **A live-capture feel.** Captured frames now carry a **sub-quality badge**, the imaging view shows a **guiding chip**, and a **meridian-flip countdown** tells you exactly how long until the flip (and it's dismissable).
- **One-shot-color stacking.** A real OSC color path — debayer → register on luminance → integrate per channel — so color-camera users get proper color integration, not just mono stacking.
- **Live stacking & stack-and-share.** Build an integration as frames come in, and produce a shareable picture from your session with one button.
- **A decluttered imaging screen.** On-image tools were grouped into a tidy toolbar, auto-hidden when you're not using them, and pulled off the image itself, so your frame isn't buried under controls. Annotation tools tuck into a dock.
- **Trustworthy annotations.** If a catalog lookup fails, you now get a real error instead of a misleading "0 objects found."
- **Cool-on-connect is off by default** — Nightshade no longer fires your camera's cooler the instant it connects.

## 🌌 Planetarium

- **Field-of-view rings** overlay your equipment's frame on the sky so you can see exactly what a target will fill.
- **Night-vision (red) mode** for dark-adapted planning.
- **Click-to-frame with smoother momentum** panning, and **touchpad fixes** so pinch-to-zoom no longer drags the view around.
- **Much smoother rendering** at higher detail — a performance rework (static/animated layer split, culling, batched drawing, projection caching) keeps panning fluid even with the full catalog loaded.
- A cleaner, modern, precision-instrument look throughout.

## ⚙️ Settings, reorganized

The flat 34-item Settings list is gone. In its place:

- **Seven logical groups in a collapsible sidebar**, so related settings sit together instead of scrolling forever.
- **A search box** at the top that filters across every setting — type "latitude," "ASTAP," "Discord," "flats," and jump straight to it.
- **Three redundant one-function pages merged** into sensible homes: **Files & Storage**, **Autofocus** (now including predictive autofocus), and **Notifications** (now including delivery routing).

Every existing "open this setting" link in the app still lands exactly where it used to.

---

## 🔌 Equipment & onboarding

- **The UI follows your hardware's real capabilities.** Cooler, rotator, pulse-guide, read-mode, binning modes, and tracking-rate options now reflect what your connected device actually reports — no more offering controls your gear doesn't have, and no more guessing a generic superset.
- **Onboarding reads your filter wheel's real slot count and names** instead of assuming five slots.
- **A guided first-launch coach** replaces the scattered first-run prompts with one progressive walkthrough, plus **post-onboarding next-step prompts** that nudge you toward framing, solving, focusing, and first light.
- **Smarter device discovery** — event-driven hot-plug detection backed by a periodic fallback, with a manual **Rescan equipment** button when you want to force a look.
- More reliable **filter-wheel, focuser, and aux-device** connections (heartbeat handling that degrades gracefully instead of dropping a working device).

- **GPU-accelerated HiPS survey tiles** — zoomable, registered real-sky imagery in the framing view that stays smooth as you pan and zoom.
- **Mosaic planning** carries straight through to the sequencer, preserving your composition and rotation.

## ☁️ Weather, safety & automation

- A live **cloud-motion** readout that predicts cloud arrival/clearing from radar.
- **Adaptive conditions** that can adjust the plan as the sky changes.
- **Safety watchdogs** wired into Smart Night plans, with weather/safety status pinned where you can see it on the cockpit.
- A hardening pass on **unattended overnight DSO runs** — a batch of fixes targeting the most failure-prone parts of an all-night automated session, so the rig is more likely to still be imaging at dawn.

## 📱 Mobile companion

- **Reach your rig from anywhere over Tailscale** — directly over your tailnet, with no relay server in the middle and no push-service dependency.
- **A reconnect fix** so the companion cleanly recovers its session after a dropped or flaky connection.

## 🐛 More fixes & polish

- **At-a-glance sequencer summaries** with **inline quick-edit** — read and tweak a node's key settings right in the sequence tree without opening its full property panel.
- **An Integrations page** for third-party connections, and **field-level help** across forms so you're never guessing what a setting does.
- **Quieter, smoother telemetry** — reduced heartbeat and discovery log spam, smoother cooler readouts, and faster device discovery.
- **Real device-capability defaults** for binning and tracking rate (they follow the device instead of a fixed list).
- Numerous stability fixes across sequencing, equipment connection, framing, and the database, and a smoother, less "AI-generated" visual style (bundled fonts, tighter spacing, quieter accents).

## 🛠️ Behind the scenes

This release also includes the largest internal cleanup Nightshade has ever shipped — a top-to-bottom refactor that breaks the biggest, most tangled parts of the codebase into focused, well-tested modules. You won't see it directly, but it means a more stable app today and faster fixes and features going forward.

## ⬆️ Upgrading

- This is a **beta-channel** build.
- **Your data carries forward.** Sequences, equipment profiles, targets, and capture history all load as-is — no action needed.
- **Your Dashboard layout migrates automatically** to the new cockpit. If you'd prefer the fresh default, reset it from the Dashboard's customize menu.
- **Start and monitor runs from the Dashboard now** — the Sequencer's "Run" tab moved there.
- **Plate-solving users:** re-confirm your ASTAP path in **Settings → Plate Solving** so solves use exactly the binary and catalog you intend.
