Specific component guidance:

NightshadeColors or local equivalent:
Replace the default dark palette with a less generic astrophotography palette. Keep semantic fields intact so the rest of the app continues to work. The dark theme should be graphite/blue-black with a restrained cyan-blue primary. The accent should be related but not saturated purple. Keep red-night mode astronomy-specific and usable.

NightshadeTokens or local equivalent:
Reduce ornamental elevation. Keep distinct surface levels, but avoid big soft shadows and glows. Use shadows primarily for true overlays, dialogs, menus, popovers, and floating elements. Let ordinary cards rely more on subtle borders and tonal contrast.

NightshadeTheme or local equivalent:
Ensure CardTheme, InputDecorationTheme, button themes, tooltip theme, divider theme, and Material ColorScheme align with the new visual language. Preserve Material 3 if already used, but avoid default Material styling that fights the custom app style.

NightshadeCard or local equivalent:
Remove hover translation. Remove top highlight gradients. Remove ordinary hover glow/elevation. Preserve selected state, but make it refined: subtle accent border, tonal selected surface, or fine left rule rather than loud glow. Keep card boundaries visible.

NightshadeButton or local equivalent:
Remove gradient primary/destructive fills. Remove hover glow. Use solid fills, subtle borders, and pressed-state darkening or tonal shift. Keep loading state. Preserve variants and sizes.

ScreenHeader / SectionHeader / SectionWell or local equivalents:
Remove decorative gradient backgrounds and gradient dividers unless extremely subtle and justified. Reduce glowing accent-bar treatment. Prefer typography-led hierarchy, fine rules, tonal grouping, and restrained active markers.

EmptyState or local equivalent:
Tone down icon tile treatment and SaaS-like presentation. Keep centered spaciousness. Make the state feel calm and operational.

StatusPill or local equivalent:
Reduce pulse and glow. Keep semantic color meaning. Make it more precise and less decorative.

Inputs/dropdowns/switches/dialogs/tooltips:
Make them consistent with the new palette and surface system. Use subtle borders, clear focus states, and restrained elevation. Preserve behavior and accessibility.

Important: do not flatten everything.
The desired result is not minimalism for its own sake. Nightshade still needs clear visual structure. Keep distinct cards, panels, status areas, grouped controls, and graph containers. The goal is refined separation, not no separation.
